### [0:30 – 1:15] Slide 2: What problem does your project solve?
*(Slide 2 displayed: Monopolistic 8-10% fee drag, custodial risk, model spoofing)*

> "Today, if you build an AI application, you likely rely on centralized aggregators like OpenRouter. But here is the dirty secret: they charge a **5.5% platform fee** on every credit purchase. Add credit card processing fees and foreign exchange markups, and AI developers are hit with an **8% to 10% fee drag**. On top of that, you must lock custodial balances that can be frozen without recourse.
> 
> Meanwhile, independent GPU data centers — including crypto mining facilities across Europe transitioning to AI — have massive idle compute. But developers don't trust them because of the *Model Spoofing* dilemma: there is currently no trustless way to prove an independent node is serving genuine, unquantized weights rather than a cheaper, degraded model."
